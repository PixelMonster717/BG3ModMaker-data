SHILI.loca无法直接编辑，一般是.xml形式编辑并封装为.loca
SHILI.xml格式一般如下：

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="utf-8"?>
<contentList>
	<content contentuid="iiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii" version="1">唱</content>
	<content contentuid="kkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkk" version="1">跳</content>
	<content contentuid="uuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuu" version="1">rap</content>
	<content contentuid="nnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnn" version="1">篮球</content>
</contentList>

其中，RootTemplate.lsf中的handle & version 应该与此处的content contentuid & version对应.
